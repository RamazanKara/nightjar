New HEVC compatibility option: Settings -> Advanced -> Decoder -> Disable HEVC low-latency flags. Off by default; omits Android and chipset low-latency flags for HEVC on any device, starting next stream. Other codecs, reference-frame invalidation and performance hints keep their settings. Decoder benchmarks honor the option and invalidate older results when it changes. Helps compare firmware-specific stutter or freezes; TV-stick hardware validation is still needed.
