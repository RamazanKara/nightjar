PyroWave integration for Nightjar

Android renderer adapted from joemossjr16/artemis-android-pyrowave
https://github.com/joemossjr16/artemis-android-pyrowave/tree/387d3a5ce1e3df8d4a4d29eec5b81a7b904926a5
GPL-3.0, as is this Moonlight-derived application (see LICENSE.txt).
Nightjar changes: protocol, capability checks, 10-bit/HDR, timing and lifecycle.

Vulkan renderer for MediaCodec streams (app/src/main/jni/vulkan)
Adapted from MoreOrLessSoftware/moonlight-android
https://github.com/MoreOrLessSoftware/moonlight-android/tree/3e6665e7d192ac5e7f639f8f8172cda04701924e
GPL-3.0, as is this Moonlight-derived application (see LICENSE.txt).
Nightjar changes: PyroWave decoding and the GPU keep-busy work left out, built against upstream
moonlight-common-c, and wired in as the "Render through Vulkan" setting.
