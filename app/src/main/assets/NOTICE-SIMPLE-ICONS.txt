Store marks from Simple Icons
=============================

The store glyphs on the TV library's cards (res/drawable/ic_store_steam, ic_store_epic,
ic_store_gog, ic_store_ea, ic_store_ubisoft, ic_store_battlenet and ic_store_xbox) are
the path data of these Simple Icons, version 16.35.0, drawn as single-colour vectors:

  steam, epicgames, gogdotcom, ea, ubisoft, battledotnet, xbox

Source: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons (https://simpleicons.org)

Simple Icons releases its icon data under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The marks themselves are trademarks of their owners (Valve, Epic Games, CD PROJEKT,
Electronic Arts, Ubisoft, Blizzard Entertainment and Microsoft). Nightjar uses them only
to name the store a game comes from, always beside or in place of the store's name, and
implies no endorsement by or affiliation with any of them.
