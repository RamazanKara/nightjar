**Lower latency and a cleaner picture on every codec.** Pairs with the [Rubylight 2.2.1 host](https://github.com/RamazanKara/Rubylight/releases).

- **Tear-free PyroWave.** Frames go to the screen through the regular swapchain, so fast motion stays clean.
- **Native means this screen.** Native resolution follows the screen the stream starts on, folded or unfolded, and the bitrate follows with it.
- **Snapdragon low-latency decoding.** Every low-latency and output-fence flag is set on Snapdragon decoders, and decoder threads run at display priority.
- **Precise decode timing.** Decode time starts the moment the decoder takes the frame.
- **Phase lock for PyroWave.** PyroWave streams report frame timing so the PC delivers each frame just before your screen refreshes, at the panel's top refresh rate. Resumed streams keep the full refresh rate.
- **HDR brightness.** When a phone reports a very low HDR peak, the PC uses its own tuned brightness default.
- **Display on the PC.** Long-press a PC > This PC > "Display on the PC": virtual display only, an additional virtual display (as main or beside the PC's own), or the physical display.
- **Overlay.** Shows "Max GPU clocks for PyroWave: On".

Tip: with VRR off, phase lock times every frame to your screen.
