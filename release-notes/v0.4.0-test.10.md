**Every stream at your screen's full resolution.** Pairs with the [Rubylight 2.2.1 host](https://github.com/RamazanKara/Rubylight/releases).

- **Native PC profiles.** A PC's own stream profile now follows your screen. "Native (follows this phone's screen)" is the first choice and the default, and profiles saved at 1920×1080 move to Native automatically.
- **Resolution source in the overlay.** The Video section shows where the stream's resolution comes from: native, the PC profile, or a fixed size.
- **Display on the PC, from the Stream settings.** Pick the PC's virtual display only, an extra virtual monitor, or the physical screen, right on the PC's Stream settings screen.
- **Sharper problem reports.** Reports include why each stream ended, the decoder and the resolution, with addresses, PINs, keys and PC names kept private.
- **Clean stream endings.** A stream the PC ends normally closes quietly.

Includes everything from test.9.
