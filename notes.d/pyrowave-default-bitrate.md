## en
- PyroWave streams no longer start at the H.264/HEVC bitrate (20 Mbps at 1080p60), far below what PyroWave needs. With the bitrate on automatic, a PyroWave stream now starts at PyroWave's own default: about 400 Mbps at 1080p60. When Nightjar knows the wired link to the PC, the default stays within 90% of it, so 2.5 and 10 Gbit/s links get the full default (1.6 Gbps at 4K60); with the link unknown, such as on Wi-Fi, it stays at or below 900 Mbps so a first stream fits ordinary gigabit Ethernet and Wi-Fi. It never goes below PyroWave's minimum. The bitrate setting shows this default while PyroWave is selected, and presets use it too. Android and desktop.
- A bitrate you choose is always kept. With PyroWave selected, the bitrate setting warns when it is below PyroWave's minimum for the resolution and frame rate (about 187 Mbps at 1080p60), where the picture breaks up. The mobile data limit still caps the bitrate.
- Nightjar's PyroWave minimum now matches the PC's (it was lower, 133 Mbps at 1080p60).
