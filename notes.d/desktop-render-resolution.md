## en
- Desktop: the PC render resolution setting (once called PC display scaling) is named, described and ranged (50-200 %) like on Android. It sets the resolution the PC renders its virtual display at, above or below the stream's; it doesn't change the size of text. A saved value above 200 % now reads as 200 %.
- Desktop: Resolution offers Android's 360p and 480p and common PC and laptop sizes (16:9, 16:10 and ultrawide up to 5120x1440), and Resolution and Frame rate each have a Custom… entry for any size or rate.
