## en
- Dolby Vision over AV1: a device with a Dolby Vision AV1 decoder, such as TCL's MediaTek TVs, now asks a Rubylight PC that offers it for Dolby Vision 10.1 and keeps AV1. Without that decoder, or from a PC that offers only Dolby Vision 8, it asks for 8.1 or 8.4 over HEVC as before. If the decoder fails, the same stream goes on as AV1 HDR10 and the overlay says so.
