## en
- Desktop: screen readers (Narrator, NVDA and JAWS on Windows, Orca on Linux) can read and use Nightjar's menus. Every button, switch and list has a name, and the PC cards and game covers read the PC's status and the game's name.
- Desktop: PIN lock, as on Android. It asks for a 4 to 8 digit PIN to open Nightjar, to start any stream, or to start a stream on selected PCs, and asks again after a time you choose. The PIN is stored only as a salted hash, and wrong tries lead to growing waits.
