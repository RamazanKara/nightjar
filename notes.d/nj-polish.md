## en
- Desktop on Windows: nightjar.exe carries the Nightjar icon, so Explorer, the taskbar and pinned shortcuts show it instead of a blank program icon ([#41](https://github.com/RamazanKara/nightjar/issues/41)).
- The PC keyboard's keys, the bitrate and other sliders, settings switches and the PCs and Settings buttons answer a tap with a light vibration, following Android's touch feedback setting ([#44](https://github.com/RamazanKara/nightjar/issues/44)).
- Profiles moved from the Video settings to the Settings overview, right under the presets, since a profile can hold values from every section ([#44](https://github.com/RamazanKara/nightjar/issues/44)).
