## en
- Settings opens each section on the settings most people change. The rest sit behind the section's last row, "More … settings", which shows them in place; search still finds every setting and opens its section at it.
- **Recover interrupted streams** is now on by default, including on installs that kept the old default. It still never starts or replaces a game; turn it off under Settings, Streaming.
- Tell the PC when on Wi-Fi, Tell the PC the link speed and the link test moved to Settings, Advanced, Diagnostics. The new-engine switch now leads Advanced, and its Experimental options have their own group.
- Labels: the USB Switch Pro driver is marked Experimental; the link test and Shared folders no longer are.
- Automatic bitrate is now called **Device controls bitrate**, in Settings, Video and in the stream menu. Off, the PC adapts the bitrate, as recommended.
