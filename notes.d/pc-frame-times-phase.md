## en
- **Show frames at the PC's frame times** keeps phase lock on, as Lowest latency does: the PC times its frames to arrive just before your screen's refresh, so frames reach the screen about 5 ms sooner in this mode. Tested on a Retroid Pocket 6 at 60 Hz: from the PC to the screen 27.8 ms (median) and 31.0 ms (95th percentile) instead of 33.0 and 41.0 ms, against 26.8 and 30.1 to 30.9 ms with Lowest latency, with as few frames more than half a refresh off as before on a steady link (0.54% against 0.51%). With simulated Wi-Fi stalls of 7 to 30 ms, slightly more frames are off than before (2.5% against 1.9%), and Lowest latency stays the smoother choice there (0.8%).
