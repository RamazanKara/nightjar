## en
- USB controllers steer the launch curtain and step or close the Control Center, expanded touch areas drag Steam trackpads smoothly, and Picture position no longer clips zoomed video or misplaces pointers.

## desktop
- Idle controllers no longer wake the launcher or stream loop at high rate when motion sensors are off or sticks have slight resting drift (#179).
- The launch curtain shows controller and keyboard button marks, takes Start+Back, Hold-Guide and the quit chord, and ignores controllers when Nightjar is in the background with background controller input off.
- Releasing a controller or opening the Control Center cancels active DualSense and DualShock touchpad touches, KMS direct scanout maps touch and pen to the letterboxed picture, and closing the Control Center restores the pointer cursor.
