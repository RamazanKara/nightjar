## en
- Windows: a new upscaling choice, AI enhancement, uses the graphics card's own video upscaler (NVIDIA RTX Video Super Resolution, Intel's video super resolution or AMD's video upscaler) for SDR and HDR streams. On NVIDIA with an HDR display it also turns SDR streams into HDR with RTX Video HDR. It is listed only when the graphics card supports it.
- Windows: HDR streams are tone mapped per frame, following the stream's HDR10+ metadata or, without it, the measured brightness of the picture, so bright and dark scenes keep their detail on displays dimmer than the content.
