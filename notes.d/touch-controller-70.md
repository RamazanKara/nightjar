## en
- On-screen controller: a new look that stays readable over bright and dark scenes. A button lights amber the moment you press it, and the sticks show their travel and deadzone.
- Controller layout editor: a control you move or resize snaps to a fine grid and lines up with the middle of the screen, with other controls and with its twin on the other side. A guide line shows what it lined up with.
- Controller layout editor: a small toolbar you can drag anywhere replaces the large panel and covers less of the layout. It adds Undo and a mirror lock that moves a control's left or right twin with it.
