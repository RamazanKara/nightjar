## en
- HDR: a screen with HLG and no HDR10 now gets HLG from a Rubylight 3.0 PC, and the overlay names the format, also with "Render through Vulkan".
- Dolby Vision (Experimental): with a Dolby Vision screen and decoder, Nightjar asks a Rubylight 3.0 PC for Dolby Vision 8.1 or 8.4. If the decoder fails, the same stream goes on as HDR10 or HLG and the overlay says so.
