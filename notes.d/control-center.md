## en
- New in a stream: the Control Center replaces the stream menu. A rail of modules (Quick, Picture, Audio, PC, Controller, Guests, Power) leads to each one's cards, with Return to stream, Disconnect and Quit game under every page. It docks at the side of the screen, or rises as a sheet on a phone held upright; a controller's triggers step through the modules.

## desktop
- New in a stream: the Control Center replaces the stream menu. It opens as a dock at the right edge with a rail of modules (Quick, Picture, Audio, PC, Controller, Guests, Power) and each module's page of cards; Return to stream, Disconnect and Quit game stay under every page. The triggers or Ctrl+Tab step through the modules, and it opens where you left it.
- The Control Center and the stream's overlays keep the night look over the game in the day and OLED themes.
- The Control Center keeps what the PC said about how the stream started at the top of Quick after its toast is gone, and Picture shows the PC's video encoder load while the PC reports it.
