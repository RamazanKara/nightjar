## en
- Microphone: echo cancellation, noise suppression, automatic gain and a gain slider. Echo cancellation is automatic by default: on with the speaker, off with headphones and Bluetooth headsets, and it follows a switch during the stream. Loud speech at high gain is rounded off instead of crackling. A device without one of these effects says so in its log instead of quietly using something else.
- Microphone button: an optional round button on the stream mutes and unmutes the microphone with one tap; drag it anywhere. Show or hide it from the stream menu.
- When the microphone settings ask for an effect the device does not have, the stream says so, and the setting reads "Not on this device".
- Modern on-screen controller: a new style beside Classic, with face buttons that take a thumb on two at once, floating or fixed sticks, a stick click by tap then touch, and an editor in the stream to move, resize, fade and hide each control for one game or all of them. Classic stays the default and keeps its layout.
