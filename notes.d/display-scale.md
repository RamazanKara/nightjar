## en
- **PC display scaling sets the text and interface size of a Rubylight virtual display**, on Android and desktop, without changing its resolution. Choose the PC's own scale (the default) or 100 % to 500 %; each PC's settings can choose its own. PC render resolution still sets how large the PC renders.
- PC display scaling needs a Rubylight host newer than 3.0.0-rc.5. Older hosts would ignore it, so that PC's settings show it as unavailable there and the app doesn't send it.
- If you raised the desktop's PC render resolution above 100 % to get bigger text, set it back to 100 % and use PC display scaling instead.
