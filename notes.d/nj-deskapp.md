## en
- Choose the controller combination that leaves the stream (Settings, Input). On Android it is off by default; on Windows and Linux Start+Select+LB+RB stays the default.
- The stream menu has a Controller mouse switch on Android and the desktop, and the desktop stream menu has a bitrate slider.
- Nightjar for Windows and Linux opens on the display and at the place it was closed, or on the main display when that one is gone.
- Nightjar for Windows writes a crash dump to its logs folder when it crashes, and checks once per launch for a newer release (it only tells you; turn it off under App).
