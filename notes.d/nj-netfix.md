## en
- A stream that never showed a picture (a blocked port, a firewall) shows its error at once instead of retrying for a minute.
- A reconnect that drops again before its first picture keeps trying, as long as the stream had shown one.
- Desktop: a PC reached through a VPN streams as remote with smaller packets that fit the tunnel. PPPoE connections stay local.
- Desktop: the stream health check says when Linux capped the receive buffer, and how to raise it.
- Windows: the control connection is now marked for network priority.
- The statistics overlay shows the bandwidth on the wire, FEC and audio included.
- A PC can be pinned to one of its addresses, and stays on it when it is found again on the network; Automatic brings back the usual choice.
- Android and Linux: audio, microphone and video packets are marked for network priority (DSCP), so routers that honor it send them first.
