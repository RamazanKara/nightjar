## en
- Desktop: Alt+F4 now goes to the game while streaming instead of closing Nightjar. The stream's own quit shortcut and the window's close button still end the stream.
- The PC's cursor no longer flickers when it changes shape: a hide waits 150 ms for the next cursor before it takes effect (desktop and Android). On desktop, scaled cursors also lose the dark fringe around their edges.
- Desktop: with clipboard sync on, files copied on this computer stay on its clipboard. Text copied on the PC no longer replaces them, and their paths are not sent to the PC.
