## en
- When the video decoder stops working and can't be brought back, for example when a TV runs out of memory, the stream now ends with a message that says what failed and what to change, instead of the app closing or the picture freezing.
- A decoder that keeps failing before it shows a picture is retried a few times with growing pauses instead of ten times in two seconds.
- After a decoder gives up, the next stream starts with safer decoder settings, as after a decoder crash, but without a second "Video decoder crashed" message or a settings reset.
