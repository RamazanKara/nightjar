## desktop
- On Windows, a USB device another driver holds (a controller, a fightstick, a flash drive) can be forwarded with usbipd-win instead of Zadig: choose Forward with usbipd-win under USB devices on the Control Center's Controller page. Windows keeps its own driver, asks for administrator rights to share the device and again to stop, and Nightjar only ever unshares what it shared itself. After a crash, the launcher asks once whether to give leftover shares back; USB devices in the Control Center offers it after that. The administrator prompt opens in front of Nightjar, a fullscreen stream too.
